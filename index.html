🐍 Python Mastery

Program Overview

A comprehensive, slide-based, day-by-day progress map designed for a 6-month (24-week) journey at 1 hour per day.

24 Weeks
1h Per Day
Zero To ML Model

Daily Routine

  • 10 Mins: Review code from yesterday.
  • 40 Mins: New Concept Lesson & Coding.
  • 10 Mins: "Deep Research" or Tinker (Break it, fix it).

Structure

5 Days Learning New Concepts

2 Days Deep Practice (Projects/Debugging)
